Zephyr Atlas 7 — Field Handbook

The Zephyr Atlas 7 is a ruggedized environmental controller for small commercial sites. This handbook is the canonical operating reference and is maintained by the Zephyr Atlas product team. It covers installation, calibration, diagnostics, and the maintenance schedule. The content below is the full article body and is intentionally longer and richer than the small "Get the form" card above it, so a correct extractor keeps this article.

Installation

Mount the controller on a dry interior wall between 1.2 and 1.6 metres from the floor. The unit accepts single-stage and two-stage heating and cooling systems and reports temperature, humidity, and runtime telemetry at five-minute intervals to the Zephyr Atlas dashboard.

Calibration

Out of the box the sensor is factory calibrated to plus or minus 0.3 degrees Celsius. Recalibrate seasonally using the two-point method described in the specification sheet.

Diagnostics

To confirm the unit is healthy, run the built-in self-test from the service menu. The firmware self-test code is ZEPHYR-4417; entering it triggers a full sensor, relay, and radio check and prints a pass or fail summary. A passing self-test ends with the token READY-OK.

Maintenance schedule

Replace the particulate filter every 90 days and the backup coin cell every 24 months. The manufacturer warranty is 36 months from the date of purchase and does not cover damage from improper installation or power surges.
