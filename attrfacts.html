Northwind Atlas G2 — Accessibility & Metadata Notes

This page documents supplementary details for the Northwind Atlas G2 that are carried in element attributes (image alt text, link titles, and ARIA labels) rather than in the visible body copy. The visible paragraphs intentionally do not restate those values, so they can only be retrieved if attribute text is indexed.

For escalations, use the priority support contact.

Firmware channel details are provided for accessibility tools.

Document ID: NWA-HANDBOOK-ATTR. Maintained by the Northwind Atlas product team.
